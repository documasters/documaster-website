Company Formation

Build the right foundation for your business.

Starting a company in the UAE involves more than obtaining a trade license. The right legal structure, business activity, jurisdiction and approval process can affect how your company operates, hires employees, opens bank accounts and expands in the future.

Our team helps entrepreneurs, investors and established businesses navigate the setup process with clear guidance from the initial consultation through registration, licensing and post-setup requirements.

Whether you are establishing a mainland company, launching in a UAE free zone or setting up a regulated professional or medical activity, we help simplify the administrative process and coordinate the required steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Business setup questions.

Can foreigners own a company in Dubai?

Ownership rules depend on the selected business activity, legal structure and jurisdiction. Our team can guide you through the available setup options based on your requirements.

Should I choose mainland or free zone?

This depends on factors such as your target market, business activity, visa requirements, office needs and planned operations. We can help compare the options before you proceed.

How long does company formation take?

Processing time varies depending on the jurisdiction, business activity, external approvals and completeness of the required documentation.

Can you also help after the company is established?

Yes. Docu Master Services can assist with PRO services, visa processing, document clearing, licensing changes, renewals and related administrative requirements.
